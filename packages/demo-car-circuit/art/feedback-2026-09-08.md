# Driving feedback — 2026-09-08

User supplied six LinkedIn comments: repeated camera discomfort, abrupt keyboard steering, and no reverse. Implemented locally; no deployment or social reply.

## Changes

- Camera position and target use one smoothed heading, with a fixed upright horizon and capped rotation (1.2 rad/s). Pause freezes the rig; switching followed worlds resets it.
- Human steering ramps more gradually and reduces steering lock at higher speed. Agent actions retain the default circuit-racing-v2 path.
- Holding Down/S brakes, waits 0.3 seconds at rest, then reverses at up to 5 m/s. Reverse may be released and reapplied without an instant stop. Reversing rail contacts use actual velocity direction.
- Human recovery button: nearest road centre in free practice; last earned checkpoint with the existing +5 s rescue penalty during a race. Countdown and completed races cannot be rescued. UI explains brake/reverse in French and English.
